About

I work on quantum computing architecture and theory as a graduate student pursuing a Ph.D. at the University of Chicago. Through my academic career I have worked in physics research characterizing properties and fabrication techniques of superconducting materials, modeling liquid helium cooling manifolds for synchrotons and linear accelerators, building a compiler toolchain for quantum computation, and performing high level system design of quantum information devices.
