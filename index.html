About

I am a research scientist working in quantum information and quantum error correction at NVIDIA Applied Research.

I previously worked on quantum information theory and simulation as a Quantum Information Scientist and Quantum Information Department Head at HRL Laboratories (now a part of IBM Research).

I received my master's degree and Ph.D. from the University of Chicago in 2020 in computer science and quantum system architecture, and a bachelor's degree physics concentrating in computer science from Cornell University in 2016. I am primarily interested in efficient quantum error correction and error mitigation protocols, quantum algorithm design and architectural optimization, and interesting ways to prove scaling properties of quantum information processing techniques. Through my academic career I have worked in research characterizing properties and fabrication techniques of superconducting materials, modeling liquid helium cooling manifolds for synchrotrons and linear accelerators, building compiler toolchains for quantum computing platforms, and performing detailed system design of quantum information processing devices.

Selected Projects

Quantum Information and Error Correction

Quantum Logic Codes: Complete Transversal Logical Clifford Instruction Sets for High-Rate Stabilizer Quantum Error Correcting Codes
arXiv 2026
Universal lower bounds on the depth needed to generate a full logical Clifford algebra, and a high-rate code family with a constant-depth transversal Clifford instruction set.
Efficient Quantum Circuits for Accurate State Preparation of Smooth, Differentiable Functions
arXiv 2020
Matrix product state methods that encode Gaussian, lognormal, and related distributions into linear-depth circuits at high fidelity.
Magic-State Functional Units: Mapping and Scheduling Multi-Level Distillation Circuits for Fault-Tolerant Quantum Architectures
MICRO 2018
First detailed hardware designs of space-time optimized magic-state factories for surface code error-corrected machines.

Quantum Algorithms

Quantum Computing for Corrosion-Resistant Materials and Anti-Corrosive Coatings Design
npj Quantum Information 2025
An end-to-end workflow and resource analysis for simulating corrosion inhibition in magnesium and niobium alloys.
Prospects for Quantum Computation in Propellant Design: Assessing the Stability of Cyclic Ozone in Nanoscale Confinement
arXiv 2024
Logical and physical overhead estimates for phase estimation of cyclic ozone confined in fullerene cages, a candidate high-impulse propellant.
Quantum Resources Required for Binding Affinity Calculations of Amyloid Beta
arXiv 2024
A computational workflow and quantum resource estimates for metal binding affinities in an intrinsically disordered protein.
